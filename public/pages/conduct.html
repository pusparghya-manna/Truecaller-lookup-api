TrueFind

Code of Conduct

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Everyone using TrueFind is expected to behave respectfully and lawfully.

Expected behaviour

  • Respect others’ privacy and consent.
  • Use lookup results only for legitimate, authorized purposes.
  • Report bugs and abuse through the channels listed in Responsible Disclosure.

Unacceptable behaviour

  • Harassment, stalking, or threats.
  • Fraud, spam, or social-engineering using lookup data.
  • Attempts to disrupt the service or compromise other users’ keys.

Violations may result in revocation of API access without notice.
