Code of Conduct

Updated Oct 13, 2026

Everyone using TrueFind is expected to act lawfully and respect other people’s privacy.

Expected

  • Use information only for legitimate, authorized purposes.
  • Respect consent and report bugs or abuse through Responsible Disclosure.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, stalking, threats, spam, fraud, or social engineering.
  • Attempts to disrupt or compromise the service.

Violations may result in access being restricted.
