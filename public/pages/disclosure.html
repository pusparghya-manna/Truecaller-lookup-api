Responsible Disclosure

Last updated: October 12, 2026

We appreciate responsible reports of security issues in TrueFind.

How to report

  • Contact @pusparghya on Telegram or open an issue / private advisory on GitHub.
  • Include steps to reproduce, impact, and any proof-of-concept if available.

Guidelines

  • Do not access or modify data that is not yours.
  • Do not perform denial-of-service testing against production without prior agreement.
  • Allow reasonable time for a fix before public disclosure.

Good-faith researchers will not face legal action for reports that follow these guidelines.
