Cookies Notice

Last updated: October 12, 2026

TrueFind uses minimal client-side storage.

What we store

  • localStorage – API key: saved only after you enter it in the popup, so you do not need to type it on every visit. It never leaves your browser except as the x-api-key header on search requests.

What we do not use

  • No advertising cookies.
  • No third-party tracking pixels.
  • No analytics cookies by default.

Clearing data

You can clear site data in your browser settings to remove the stored API key.
