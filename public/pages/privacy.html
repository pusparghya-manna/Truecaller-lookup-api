Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

TrueFind is a private lookup interface operated by Pusparghya. This notice explains what data is handled when you use the service.

What we process

  • Phone numbers you submit for lookup.
  • API key stored only in your browser (localStorage) and sent to the server as an authentication header.
  • Technical logs such as request time and status codes, used to keep the service reliable.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not store your API key on the server beyond the request.
  • Lookup results are returned to you and are not retained as a public directory by this site.

Third parties

Lookups are performed through an authenticated session with third-party directory services. Their own privacy terms also apply to data they hold.

Contact

Questions: reach out via Telegram @pusparghya or GitHub @pusparghya-manna.
