Privacy Policy

TrueFind is an independently operated interface for authorized caller lookups.

What is handled

Numbers you submit and basic request details, such as time and status, are processed to provide and maintain the service.

Directory providers

Authorized lookups are sent to third-party directory providers. Their privacy and retention practices apply to data they hold.

We do not sell personal data or keep lookup results as a public directory.
